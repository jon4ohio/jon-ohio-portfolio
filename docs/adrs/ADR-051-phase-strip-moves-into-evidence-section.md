# ADR-051: Phase strip lives inside Evidence (remove Spine from chapter nav)

## Status
<!-- One of: Draft | Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-NNN -->
**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** 2026-04-30
**Decision Maker(s):** John Ohio
**Supersedes:** None

## Context

Flagship case studies currently present both:\n- A “Spine/Phases” section (with the phase strip) before Evidence, and\n- An “Evidence” section whose content is organized by those same phases.\n\nIn practice, the phase strip is not a separate step — it is a navigation aid that helps readers jump between evidence sections anchored by phase IDs.\n\nKeeping “Spine/Phases” as a separate chapter creates redundancy in the sticky chapter nav and suggests two distinct steps where there is only one.\n\n**In scope:** Information architecture for flagship case study navigation; placement of the phase strip.\n**Out of scope:** Visual redesign of the strip, changes to evidence content, non-flagship case studies.\n+
## Decision Drivers
\n- Reduce redundancy: phase strip and evidence are the same step.\n- Improve clarity: chapter nav should reflect major sections only.\n- Preserve behavior: phase strip continues to link to the same `#id` anchors.\n\n## Options Considered\n\n### Option A: Keep Spine and Evidence separate\n- **Description:** Leave the strip in its own “Spine” section and keep a separate “Evidence” chapter.\n- **Pros:** No refactor.\n- **Cons:** Redundant; implies two different steps; extra scrolling.\n- **Effort:** Low\n- **Notes:** Confuses readers about where evidence begins.\n\n### Option B: Move phase strip into Evidence (chosen)\n- **Description:** Remove the standalone Spine section and render the phase strip at the start of the Evidence section.\n- **Pros:** Single, coherent step; strip appears exactly where it’s needed; chapter nav is cleaner.\n- **Cons:** Loses the separate “Spine” heading block (copy) unless reintroduced elsewhere.\n- **Effort:** Low\n- **Notes:** Phase strip still links to the same phase anchor IDs.\n\n## Decision\n\n**We will render the phase strip inside the Evidence section and remove the “Spine/Phases” chapter from the sticky chapter nav for flagship case studies.**\n\n## Consequences\n\n### Positive\n- Chapter nav matches the actual user journey.\n- Evidence navigation is colocated with evidence.\n- Fewer redundant sections.\n\n### Negative / Trade-offs\n- The standalone “Spine” explanatory heading/subhead is removed; if needed, it should be expressed as short copy inside Evidence.\n\n### Operational Impact\n- **Migration / rollback:** Re-add `FlagshipSpine` above Evidence and restore a “phases” chapter in `StickyChapterNav`.\n\n### Risks\n\n| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner/Role | Review Trigger |\n|------|-----------|--------|------------|------------|----------------|\n| Readers miss the “Spine” explanatory framing when it’s removed | Med | Low/Med | Add a short 1–2 sentence intro above the strip inside Evidence if needed | John Ohio | Any flagship narrative revision |\n\n## Review Schedule\n\n- **Next review:** Next flagship IA review\n- **Review owner:** John Ohio\n\n## Related ADRs\n\n- ADR-049 — relates to: navigation and tablet stability decisions\n\n## References\n\n- `components/case-study/StickyChapterNav.tsx`\n- `components/case-study/PhaseTimeline.tsx`\n- `app/work/seamless-hiring/page.tsx`\n- `app/work/seamkit/page.tsx`\n- `app/work/ibedc/page.tsx`\n- `app/work/fetsproza/page.tsx`\n+
